Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Overview

Outlook Gmail Sync is a personal, self-hosted email synchronization application.

Information accessed

The application accesses email messages and attachments from an authorized Microsoft Outlook.com mailbox and imports them into an authorized Gmail mailbox.

How information is used

Email data is used solely to copy messages between the user's authorized accounts.

Storage and security

OAuth authentication tokens and synchronization metadata are stored locally on the user's server. Email message contents are processed during transfer and are not intentionally retained in a separate permanent message archive.

Data sharing

The application does not sell user data or share email information with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Data retention and deletion

The user controls the self-hosted server and can delete locally stored OAuth tokens and synchronization records. Email copies already imported into Gmail remain subject to the user's Gmail account controls.

Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the developer using the support email displayed on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
